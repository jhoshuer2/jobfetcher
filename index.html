How the 10-day rule works: every listing keeps a history of the dates it was advertised. Its age is counted from the most recent advertisement. Past 10 days it's removed; if the same company posts the same role again, it returns with a "Re-advertised" tag.
Pay: "Pay confirmed" means the listing states $1,000+/month (yearly and hourly pay are converted; hourly assumes full-time). Listings with no pay stated are kept but tagged "Pay not listed". Anything stated below $1,000/month is hidden.
Live sources: Remotive, Jobicy, Remote OK and Himalayas public job APIs, fetched from your browser (auto-refresh every 6 hours when open). Some may be blocked by the browser; the verified picks still show. Data is saved in this browser only.
